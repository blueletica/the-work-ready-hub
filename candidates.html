How Candidate Registration Works

A simple way to introduce your professional background and be considered for potential career opportunities.

1. Complete Your Registration

Tell us about your professional experience, preferred job categories, location, and the types of opportunities you are seeking.

2. Submit Your CV

Upload your most recent CV so we can better understand your qualifications, skills, and professional background.

3. Profile Review

Candidate information may be reviewed to identify potential matches with suitable employment opportunities.

4. Potential Opportunities

If your profile matches a suitable opportunity, we may contact you to discuss the position and next steps.

Your Information. Your Privacy.

We believe candidates deserve transparency, respect, and control over their personal information.

Confidential Registration

Your personal information and CV are collected for recruitment-related purposes and stored through our form provider, Tally. We aim to handle your information responsibly and restrict access to those who need it.

Permission Before Sharing

Your CV and personal information will not be shared with recruitment partners or potential employers without your permission for the specific opportunity.

Manage Your Information

You can request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct outdated details, or request deletion of your CV and information.

Need to Update or Remove Your Information?

If you've previously registered with The Work Ready Hub, you can submit a privacy request at any time.

Submit a Privacy Request

Privacy requests are completely free.
